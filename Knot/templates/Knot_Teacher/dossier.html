About

Public

I've spent ten years building product frontends for teams that couldn't afford to guess — fintech, health, and infrastructure. Along the way I realised that the hardest problems were never about the framework. They were about clarity.

Now I teach the same thing I had to learn the hard way: how to design systems that stay readable at 100,000 lines, how to write TypeScript that catches bugs before your users do, and how to hold the line on craft when the deadline is closer than it should be.

"The best code isn't the cleverest one. It's the one a stranger can read at 2 a.m. and still fix."

My courses are practical, opinionated, and built from real production decisions — not toy examples. If you want to know why a pattern exists before you use it, you'll feel at home here.
