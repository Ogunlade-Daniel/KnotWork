How honours work

Faculty charter · Revised Jan 2026
Article I

Thresholds, not opinions

Every distinction maps to a published numeric threshold — hours taught, completion rate, review count. Progress is calculated nightly from platform records.

Article II

XP is transparent

XP is awarded by a fixed schedule across five categories. Your ledger shows exactly where every point came from, and it never expires mid-season.

Article III

Awards are auditable

Each credential carries a unique ID and can be verified publicly. Awards may be retired if the underlying threshold is no longer met — always with a written reason.

Article IV

The right to appeal

Disagree with a decision? Lodge an appeal within 30 days. It is reviewed by the Faculty Council and answered in writing, either way.
