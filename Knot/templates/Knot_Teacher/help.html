Frequently asked questions

Getting started

Your profile is what students see when they discover you. To get to 100% complete, add a profile photo, a short bio under 320 characters, an expertise set of at least three tags, and connect a bank account for payouts.

  1. Go to Settings → Profile.
  2. Upload a square profile photo (minimum 400×400px).
  3. Write a short bio — one or two sentences about your craft.
  4. Add at least three expertise tags so students can find you.
  5. Under Account, verify your email and connect a bank account.

Profiles above 90% completion get roughly 2.4× more enrolments than incomplete ones.

Levels reflect your cumulative XP across teaching, ratings, completions, community and authoring. There are six tiers:

  • Apprentice — Level I–IV
  • Journeyman — Level V–VII
  • Artisan — Level VIII–X
  • Senior — Level XI
  • Master — Level XII–XIII
  • Laureate — Level XIV+

Your current tier unlocks perks like reduced platform fees (12% at Master vs. 20% standard), priority studio slots and a verified faculty crest on your public profile.

Most faculty verifications complete within 24–48 hours. We review your public portfolio, teaching sample, and any linked professional profiles. You'll receive an email the moment your crest goes live.

If your verification is taking longer than 72 hours, please open a support ticket with the Account category and we'll investigate.

Courses & content

A course moves through three states: Draft → Review → Live. You can edit a draft freely. Once submitted for review, our team checks the outline, the sample lesson, and the completion criteria.

  1. Go to Atelier → Compose course.
  2. Add a title, one-paragraph description, and at least six modules.
  3. Record or upload a two-minute intro video.
  4. Submit for review — turnaround is typically under 3 days.

You'll be notified at each step, and you can pull back a submitted draft at any time before approval.

Yes. Small edits (typos, video replacements, resource updates) publish instantly. Structural changes — adding or removing modules, changing pricing — create a new version. Students enrolled before the change stay on their version and finish with the criteria they agreed to.

You'll see both versions in the course editor, and can compare them side by side.

There's no limit on the number of modules, but individual video uploads are capped at 2 GB and 4 hours. If you need longer lessons, split them into parts — which is better pedagogy anyway.

For large cohorts, we recommend keeping individual lessons under 20 minutes. Session completion rates drop sharply after that.

Students & cohort

You have several tools, escalating in severity:

  • Mute — stop them messaging you or others in a session.
  • Remove from session — kicks them from the current live room.
  • Unenroll — removes them from the course entirely. They receive a full refund; you keep 40% of the fee as compensation for the disruption.
  • Report — flags the account to the Faculty Council for review.

All actions are logged and reviewed. You will never be penalised for using them in good faith.

Yes, if the destination cohort has capacity and the student is in good standing (no unpaid balances, no active disputes). Transfers preserve their progress and grades. Both you and the destination instructor receive an email notification.

Students get one free transfer per course. Subsequent transfers incur a small administrative fee, which is split between both instructors.

Earnings & payouts

Payouts run on the 1st of each month, covering the previous calendar month's cleared earnings. Your platform fee depends on your Level:

  • Apprentice / Journeyman — 20%
  • Artisan / Senior — 15%
  • Master — 12%
  • Laureate — 8%

Payments settle in 1–3 business days depending on your bank. If a payout fails, the amount is automatically held and retried the next business day.

Your annual earnings statement and any applicable 1099-NEC are generated automatically and stored under Earnings → Documents. Both are ready by 31 January each year.

If your jurisdiction requires additional forms, our finance team will reach out directly. You can also request a custom summary any time from the same panel.

Pending earnings come from recent enrolments or sessions that are still within the 14-day refund window. They clear automatically once that window passes without a refund request.

You can see a full breakdown of pending amounts in the Ledger tab of your Earnings page, including the exact date each amount clears.

Your personal library of courses and drafts. The place where you compose new work.

The live teaching room. Where sessions happen with real students, real-time.

XP

Experience points earned across five categories. The unit of the Faculty Ladder.

Credential

A verifiable, dated award with a unique ID. Can be checked publicly by anyone.

Earnings inside the 14-day refund window. Cleared automatically once it passes.
