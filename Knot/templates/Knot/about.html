Knotwork: Connect, learn, and grow together

Our platform empowers learners and mentors worldwide with real-time voice and video collaboration, project-based study rooms, donation support, and verified learner reviews.

International learning ecosystem

Mission

Knotwork brings people together in a safe online space where education, mentorship, and community overlap. Users can discover peers, join live rooms, give feedback through reviews, and fund the platform so it continues growing globally.

What makes Knotwork world-class?

Live communication

Instant voice and video rooms reduce friction and make peer learning feel natural.

Global collaboration

Learn with participants across continents, share skills, and gain real-world experience.

Trust through reviews

Every review helps learners choose the best sessions and helps mentors improve their delivery.

Support the movement

Donations keep the project free, secure, and adaptable as the platform scales.

How it works

  • Create a learner or mentor profile with your interests.
  • Browse live rooms by skill area, format, or language.
  • Join sessions instantly or schedule collaborative workshops.
  • Share feedback and help the community grow with reviews.
  • Donate to fund new features and keep the platform accessible.

Built for learners, by learners — Knotwork is your gateway to shared discovery.
