Full knowledge base

Knotwork is a skill exchange platform. Instead of paying for every lesson, you teach what you know and learn what you want in return — a fair two-way swap.

Here's how it flows:

  1. Build your profile — list the skills you can teach and the ones you want to learn.
  2. Get matched — we only show you people where the swap works both ways.
  3. Swipe & connect — send a request. If they accept, you're connected.
  4. Book & swap — schedule a session and start teaching and learning.
Tip: the more precise your skill tags, the better your matches. Vague tags like "coding" produce weak matches; "React" and "Figma" produce great ones.

Skill exchanges are always free. When you teach and learn in a balanced way, no credits change hands — you simply swap time.

Credits are used when:

  • You book a session with a mentor who charges for their time.
  • You enrol in a structured course on the platform.
  • You want a paid session without offering a swap in return.

You earn credits by teaching sessions as a mentor, referring friends, and hitting streaks. Learn more about credits.

You must be 16 or older to create an account. Members between 16 and 18 need parental consent to use paid features and mentorship programmes.

We welcome learners of all levels — beginners, career-switchers, and seasoned professionals. Everyone starts somewhere, and teaching a beginner is often the best way to deepen your own understanding.

Every match is scored on a two-way basis. That means we only show you someone if:

  • They teach at least one skill on your "want to learn" list, and
  • You teach at least one skill on their "want to learn" list.

The match score (0–100%) considers:

  • How well your skills overlap (35%)
  • Schedule compatibility (20%)
  • Experience level fit (15%)
  • Language and timezone (10%)
  • Past feedback and reviews (20%)
Profiles with 3 or more precise skill tags and a filled-out bio get 4× more matches than sparse profiles.

You are never locked into anything. You can:

  • Decline any request — silently and permanently. The other person is never told you said no.
  • Remove a connection at any time from your Connections page.
  • Block a member — they won't see your profile, and you won't see theirs in matches.
  • Report a problem — our moderation team reviews every report within 24 hours.

Your decisions here are private. Nobody gets notified when you decline, remove or block.

Yes. Knotwork is fully responsive and works on any modern browser. We also have:

  • iOS app — available on the App Store (requires iOS 15+).
  • Android app — available on Google Play (requires Android 10+).

The mobile apps support push notifications, so you get session reminders and match alerts on the go.

For the best experience, use the app for sessions and messages, and the web version for creating content and editing skills.
  1. Go to Skill Matches and find someone whose skills overlap with yours.
  2. Send a connection or exchange request. Wait for them to accept.
  3. Once connected, go to Sessions and click Book a session.
  4. Choose a topic, duration, and time from their availability.
  5. You'll both get a confirmation, plus a reminder 15 minutes before.

If a mentor doesn't join within 10 minutes of the session start time, we automatically:

  • Release the session from your schedule.
  • Refund the full credits to your wallet.
  • Log a no-show on the mentor's profile (this affects their search ranking).

Repeated no-shows result in review by our moderation team. You can also mark a session as a no-show manually if our automatic system misses it.

Yes, and it's always free if you do it more than 24 hours in advance. Here's the policy:

  • More than 24h before: free reschedule or full refund, no penalty.
  • Under 24h: you may be charged 25% of the session cost.
  • No-show: full session cost is charged.
Regular last-minute cancellations may impact your reliability score, which mentors can see when deciding whether to accept your requests.

By default, sessions are not recorded unless both parties agree in writing before the session starts.

If you do consent to recording:

  • The recording is available for 30 days in your session history.
  • Both participants can download or delete it at any time.
  • Recordings can never be shared or posted without both parties' permission.

You only need:

  • A stable internet connection (5 Mbps+ recommended).
  • A camera and microphone — built into most laptops and phones.
  • A modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge) — no download required.

Sessions run in our built-in video room, which is encrypted end-to-end and doesn't require any app or plugin.

Credits are earned by giving back to the community. You can earn them by:

  • Teaching sessions: 40 credits per completed 45-minute session.
  • Leaving reviews: 10 credits for honest, helpful feedback.
  • Referring friends: 150 credits when they complete their first session.
  • Hitting streaks: 25 credits per week of consistent learning.
  • Community contributions: 5–20 credits per high-quality post or answer.

Refunds are simple and automatic in most cases:

  • Mentor no-show: 100% refund, processed within 5 minutes.
  • You cancel >24h ahead: 100% refund, instant.
  • You cancel <24h ahead: 75% refund (25% cancellation fee).
  • Course purchase: 100% refund within 7 days if you've viewed less than 20% of the content.

Refunds are returned as credits to your wallet. If you paid by card, you can request a cash refund from your wallet page within 30 days.

Credits do not expire as long as your account remains active. If your account is inactive for 24 months, we'll send you a reminder before any expiry applies.

Credits cannot be transferred between accounts, and cannot be exchanged for cash except in specific regions — you'll see the option on your wallet page if it's available to you.

All payments are processed securely by Stripe. We accept:

  • Visa, Mastercard, and American Express
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay
  • Bank transfer (for top-ups over $100)
We never store your full card number. Only the last four digits are kept for your reference — the rest is handled entirely by Stripe.
  1. Go to Settings → Security.
  2. Enter your current password.
  3. Enter and confirm your new password (minimum 10 characters).
  4. Click Update password.

All other active sessions will be signed out automatically for safety. You'll receive a confirmation email.

  1. Go to Settings → Security.
  2. Click Manage next to Authenticator app.
  3. Scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, 1Password, or Authy.
  4. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm.
  5. Save your backup codes in a safe place — you'll need them if you lose your phone.
2FA is strongly recommended, especially if you're a mentor. Accounts with 2FA enabled are 99.9% less likely to be compromised.

Email us at support@knotwork.io from any address, with:

  • Your username or profile URL.
  • The old email address on the account.
  • Approximate date of last sign-in and recent transactions (if any).
  • A photo ID (this is required for security).

We'll verify your identity and help you restore access, usually within 24–48 hours.

Yes. Head to Settings → Danger zone and choose Delete my account.

When you delete your account:

  • Your profile, sessions, credits and connections are permanently removed.
  • Anonymous analytics data may be retained for up to 12 months for platform statistics.
  • Anything you posted publicly in community may be anonymised rather than deleted, so existing threads remain readable.

You can export all your data before deleting from the same settings page.

Your safety comes first. Please:

  1. End the session or conversation immediately if you feel unsafe.
  2. Go to that person's profile and click Report.
  3. Choose the reason (harassment, spam, inappropriate content, etc.).
  4. Optionally, block them — you'll never see each other again.

Every report is reviewed by our moderation team within 24 hours. If a member is found in violation, their account may be suspended or terminated.

Never share personal payment details, home address, or government ID with anyone on Knotwork. We never ask for those.

To block: visit their profile and click the ⋯ menu → Block. This is silent — they will never know you blocked them.

To report: visit their profile and click ⋯ menu → Report. Add as much detail as you can (screenshots help).

Blocking is immediate. Reporting may lead to a review and, if warranted, account action.

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account: name, email, password hash, and optional profile photo.
  • Activity: sessions, connections, and progress — to run matches.
  • Billing: handled entirely by Stripe — we never see your full card number.
  • Device: IP and browser information for security and fraud prevention.

We never sell your personal data, and we never share your email or location with other members unless you explicitly enable that in your privacy settings.

You can export everything we hold on you at any time from Settings → Privacy.

Yes. All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest:

  • In transit: TLS 1.3 with HSTS enabled.
  • At rest: AES-256 encryption.
  • Video sessions: End-to-end encrypted with ephemeral keys.
  • Passwords: Hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text.

We are SOC 2 Type II compliant, and independently audited annually.
