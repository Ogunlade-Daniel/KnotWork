Full knowledge base

You can reschedule or cancel any session directly from the Sessions page. Click the Reschedule or Cancel action on the session card.

  • Reschedules require mentee confirmation — they get 24 hours to accept.
  • Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the session incur no fee.
  • Late cancellations (under 24 hours) incur a 10% fee deducted from the session payout.
  • Emergency cancellations can be reviewed by support and refunded.

Repeat late cancellations affect your attendance rate, which is displayed on your public dossier.

Go to Sessions Refund policy

Payouts are processed automatically on your chosen schedule — by default, on the 1st of each month.

  • A session becomes payable once both you and the mentee mark it complete.
  • Funds clear into your available balance 24 hours after completion.
  • Payouts to your verified bank arrive in 1–3 business days and are free.
  • PayPal payouts arrive within 24 hours with a 1.5% fee.
  • You can also withdraw any time manually from the Earnings page.

Knotwork charges a 15% platform fee on every completed session — this is the only deduction and it's shown on every transaction.

Two-factor authentication is strongly recommended for every mentor, and it's required for anyone with an active payment method.

To enable 2FA:

  • Go to Settings → Security.
  • Click Manage next to Two-factor authentication.
  • Scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, 1Password, or Authy.
  • Enter the 6-digit code to confirm.

If you've lost your device: use one of your 10 backup codes, or open a support ticket from a recognised device. Support will verify your identity before resetting 2FA.

You can pause new requests while staying visible to current mentees.

  • Go to Settings → Availability.
  • Toggle "Accepting new mentees" off.
  • Your profile stays public; a small "Not accepting requests" pill appears.

Any requests received before you paused remain in your queue — you can still accept or decline them.

Level advancement is based on a weighted combination of four factors:

  • Total session hours completed.
  • Average rating across all reviews (minimum 4.5).
  • Response rate — replies to messages within 24 hours.
  • Community contribution — forum answers and accepted solutions.

Badges are issued by the faculty board when the criteria for each badge are met. You can see the criteria for every badge on your Honours page.

Go to Honours See your metrics

Disputes are rare, and they're always handled fairly.

  • When a mentee opens a dispute, the session's payout is held in escrow — it is not removed from your account.
  • Support reviews the session recording, chat history, and both parties' notes.
  • You have 48 hours to submit your side of the story.
  • A decision is issued within 5 business days, and both parties are notified in writing.

Earnings that have already cleared into your available balance are never reversed except in cases of confirmed fraud.

The Resources page is your file library. You can share:

  • PDFs, images, videos, code files, sheets, and external links.
  • Individual files up to 50 MB each.
  • Each resource with as many mentees as you want, or the whole cohort.
  • Access can be revoked at any time — it disappears from their dashboard instantly.

Files are encrypted at rest and backed up across three regions. Deleted files are retained in an archive for 30 days.

You have two options, both available from Settings → Security → Danger Zone.

  • Deactivate — your profile is hidden and you stop receiving requests, but your data is retained. Reactivate any time.
  • Delete — your account is permanently removed after a 30-day retention window.

Before you can delete, you must have no pending payouts and no active mentees mid-plan. Support will help you wind down gracefully if needed.

Some records — completed payouts, signed agreements, and legal disclosures — are retained for 7 years to comply with tax law.
