About Daniel

Edit bio

I'm a senior frontend and platform engineer with 12 years of production experience across fintech, developer tooling, and consumer web. I've led frontend architecture for products used by millions, built design systems, and mentored engineers at every level from junior to staff.

Good mentoring isn't about teaching a stack — it's about turning an engineer's thinking from “how do I do this?” into “how do I decide what's worth doing?”

My sessions are structured, honest, and practical. Every plan starts with a clear goal, every session ends with a written action list, and every milestone is measurable. I bring the same rigor I use in production code reviews to every conversation with a mentee.

If you're an intermediate engineer ready to move into senior scope — architecture decisions, cross-team communication, performance work, hiring interviews — I'm probably a good fit. If you're starting from zero, I'm not the fastest path, and I'll tell you that honestly.
